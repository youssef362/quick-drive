      FAQs

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How much does it cost to rent a car in Dubai?+
      Economy cars start from AED 70/day, AED 420/week, or AED 1,380/month at Quick Drive. SUV and luxury rates depend on the model. All prices include standard insurance. Pay online at the time of booking and get 10% off instantly.
      Can tourists rent a car in Dubai?+
      Yes. Visitors need a valid passport, visit visa with entry stamp, a home-country driving licence, and an International Driving Permit if the licence isn’t in English or Arabic. There’s no minimum stay requirement.
      Do I need to pay a security deposit?+
      Most Quick Drive vehicles require a refundable deposit of AED 500-2000, depending on car models and the duration, held on your.
      Is insurance included in the rental price?+
      Standard insurance is included with every booking. For extra peace of mind, add Super CDW at AED 25/day or AED 250/month covering excess, windows, mirrors, wheels and tyres.
      Can I rent a car in Dubai and drive to other emirates?+
      Absolutely. All our cars are cleared for travel across the UAE — Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, RAK, Fujairah, and UAQ. Salik and Darb tolls apply as standard.
      What happens if the car breaks down?+
      Call us. Our roadside team operates 24/7 across the UAE. We’ll either repair the car on the spot or deliver a replacement — usually within a couple of hours.
      Do I get charged for Salik tolls?+
      All our cars have Salik stickers. Each toll gate crossing costs AED 5, and the total is deducted from your security deposit at the end. No admin fees on top.
      Can someone else drive my rental car?+
      The car is registered to the primary driver. Additional drivers cost AED 15/day or AED 150/month. They’ll need to submit the same documents as the primary renter.
      Is monthly car rental cheaper than buying?+
      For stays under two years, almost always. Monthly rental from AED 1,380 covers insurance, maintenance, registration, and roadside support. Buying adds loan repayments, insurance premiums, service costs and depreciation.
      How do I book a car with Quick Drive?+
      Three ways: browse our fleet at quickdrive.ae/cars-for-rent and book online, call us on +971 50 525 9245, or WhatsApp the same number. We’ll confirm availability and have you driving the same day.
